# Lumen PDF reader

Readers keep PDFs in a folder on their computer, browse them in the app, and mark places they want to revisit.

## Language

**PDF folder**

The folder on the reader's computer that holds the PDFs shown in the book selector.

**Book selector**

A pop-up opened by a button. The reader scrolls vertically through first-page previews and picks a PDF to open.

**Bookmark**

A place the reader deliberately marks in a PDF so they can return to it, including after closing the PDF and restarting the app.

**Reading position**

The page where the reader last left a book. It moves as the reader turns pages, independently of any bookmarks.

**Page appearance**

The colors Lumen uses to display a PDF page while reading. The original page colors remain available when the reader switches back.

**Single-page view**

A reading view that shows one PDF page at a time. A page turn replaces the visible page with the next or previous one.
